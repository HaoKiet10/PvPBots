# PvP Bots v3 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add rating-balanced team rounds with bot fill to `squad`/`team` arenas, and plugin-built boss arenas with souls-like fake-player bosses, configurable movesets, phases and personal rewards.

**Architecture:** Same split as v1/v2: pure Kotlin state machines and rules in `core` (team split, team round, boss encounter, move selection, health pool, loot) unit-tested without a server; Bukkit/WorldGuard glue in `core/bukkit`; no adapter changes. Team rounds are a new session beside `TrainerSession` in `AreaDirector`; bosses are a separate `BossDirector` with its own controller and brain on top of the existing fake-player spawn path.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin 2.x (core JVM 21), Gradle Kotlin DSL, Paper API (BossBar, particles, sounds, projectiles), WorldGuard 7, JUnit 5 + kotlin.test, run-paper self-tests.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-19-pvp-bots-v3-design.md` (on top of the v1 and v2 specs; v2 plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-18-pvp-bots-v2.md` describes the newest code). Branch `feat/v3-teams-bosses` (from v2's `feat/v2-arenas-adaptive-26_3`).

## Global Constraints

- All v1/v2 Global Constraints still hold (Kotlin everywhere, NMS only in adapters, no player commands, bots never saved, ≤ 10 ms/tick for 100 bots, players never lose their own items through the plugin, terrain always restorable).
- **No adapter changes**: boss effects use the Bukkit/Paper API from `core`.
- Team rounds: only built arenas with preset `squad` (3 per side) or `team` (5 per side). 1 player → v2 1v1 trainer unchanged; ≥ 2 players → team round; a running 1v1 ends with **NO_RESULT** when a 2nd player enters.
- Split: sort by rating desc; each to the team with the lower rating total that has a free seat; ties → team A. Bots fill every empty seat; filler bot rating = **its team's average player rating**, fixed for the round.
- If filler bots can't be seated within **10 s**, shrink to the largest even size that fits; players-only only if the split is even; otherwise keep waiting.
- No friendly fire. Mid-round entrants wait (action bar `Next round starts soon`); waiting players go first next round. Dead or departed players are out for the round.
- Titles: `Your team won!` / `Your team lost` / `Time's up — draw`. Team ratings: `RatingUpdate.after(old, old, outcome)` (PLAYER_WON / BOT_WON / DRAW for winners / losers / draw).
- Boss commands (permission `pvpbots.admin`): `/pvpbots boss create <name> <boss-type> [confirm]`, `/pvpbots boss remove <name> [confirm]`, `/pvpbots boss list`.
- Boss preset: round, inside radius **20**, walls **6** high, cleared to wall height + **4**, 3-wide entrance, no cover, lights in floor; region `pvpbots_<name>` priority **10**, `pvpbot-role boss`, `pvpbot-boss <type>`, `build deny`, `pvp allow`; plugin blocks real player ↔ real player damage in boss arenas.
- Boss health pool in the plugin: `base-health × (1 + health-per-extra-player × (players − 1))` at INTRO (default per-extra **0.6**); raised (fraction kept) when players join; never lowered. Fake player's own health kept full; hits cancelled and subtracted from the pool, **×1.5** during recovery, ignored from outside the region and during a roar.
- Encounter: INTRO **3 s**; RESET when no participant for **10 s**; COOLDOWN default **600 s**, nobody ejected, action bar `The arena is quiet… returns in m:ss`; victory title `BOSS DEFEATED`. The boss uses one `max-bots` slot.
- Participant = survival/adventure, alive, inside the region. Winner = dealt ≥ 1 damage this fight AND (inside at death OR died inside during this fight).
- Every damaging special move: telegraph ≥ **10** ticks, damage only at tell end. Cheese guards: immune to fall/fire/lava/drowning/suffocation; back to centre if it leaves the region; leap or ranged if no target reachable on foot for **3 s** or a target is > **4** blocks above; never idle > **3 s** with a living target. Aggro: most damage in the last **10 s**, with randomness. Bosses never target bots; trainers/allies never fight bosses.
- Rewards per winner: loot (`guaranteed`, `rolls`, `table` of item/amount/chance), overflow at feet; `xp`; `commands` from console with `%player%`/`%boss%`; `BossDefeatedEvent(arena, bossType, winners)` fired before rewards (not cancellable). No first-kill bonus.
- Example boss types `ashblade` and `thornbow` as in spec §5.2.

## Review Focus

1. **Players leave, die or log out mid team round, including the last player on a side** → the round resolves (a side with no players and no bots loses) and never sticks. Test: Task 1 `lastPlayerOfSideLeaves_otherSideWins`, `allPlayersLeave_roundEndsNoResult`.
2. **A team round in a kit arena** → each player is stashed/kitted/restored exactly as v2, teleport to pads doesn't break the stash. Test: Task 3 self-test `T9 team round in kit arena`.
3. **Everyone logs out or the server restarts mid boss fight** → the boss is removed, no rewards, arena back to IDLE (no stuck boss bar, no orphan bot). Test: Task 4 `allParticipantsGone_resetsAfter10s`; Task 6 self-test `B5 wipe resets`.
4. **Hits on the boss from outside the region (arrows, tridents)** → ignored. Test: Task 6 self-test `B7 cheese guards`.
5. **`/pvpbots reload` or `clear` during a team round or boss fight** → all bots removed, no ratings changed, no rewards, sessions restart cleanly. Test: Task 2 `shutdown_duringTeamRound_noResults`; Task 6 `B8 reload during fight`.
